This does not mean traditional beliefs are simply “monster stories”. Academic studies of PNG folklore have shown how supernatural figures can evolve over time, sometimes becoming symbols of identity, history, or modern concerns rather than fixed ancient legends.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/339562" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journals.uchicago.edu">[University of Chicago Press Journals]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journals.uchicago.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">University of Chicago Press JournalsFore Narratives Through Time How a Bush Spirit Became a Robber, Was Sent to Jail, Emerged as the Symb...</span></span></span>

## The Boianai UFO: Papua New Guinea’s most famous sky mystery

The most internationally famous PNG anomaly occurred in 1959 at the Anglican mission station of Boianai in what was then the Territory of Papua and New Guinea. Reverend William Gill reported seeing unusual lights and objects in the sky, followed by a dramatic observation on 26 June when he and many others described a large disc-like object above the mission. Reports claimed that figures appeared on the object and that one appeared to respond when Gill waved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.usufocenter.com/ufo-sighting-reports/worldwide/papua-new-guinea-ufo-sightings.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: usufocenter.com">[US UFO Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">usufocenter.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">US UFO Center Global UFOs: Papua New Guinea UFO Sightings &amp; Reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">US UFO Center Global UFOs: Papua New Guinea UFO Sightings &amp; Reports</span></span></span>

The case attracted attention because it was not based on a single anonymous witness. Gill recorded events, collected statements, and reported that many local residents and mission workers had seen unusual objects. Australian government records also contain references to unidentified aerial reports in the region during this period, showing that the wider wave of sightings was taken seriously enough to be documented.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.project1947.com/kbcat/kb_uasgov.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: project1947.com">[project1947.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">project1947.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">kb uasgov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PROJECT 1947: “UNUSUAL AERIAL SIGHTINGS” A SEARCH THROUGH THE AUSTRALIAN GOVERNMENT’S RECORDS SYSTEMSJanuary 1, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 1, 2016</span></span></span>

### What explanations have been proposed?

UFO researchers have often presented Boianai as one of the strongest historical “close encounter” cases because of the number of witnesses and the presence of written records. Some later accounts have treated it as evidence of extraterrestrial visitation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufonews.co/post/priest-sees-ufo-papua-new-guinea" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufonews.co">[UFO News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufonews.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO News Priest Sees UFO, Waves, and Receives a Startling Response</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO NewsPriest Sees UFO, Waves, and Receives a Startling Response - Papua New GuineaNovember 24, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 24, 2024</span></span></span>

Sceptical explanations focus on more ordinary possibilities. Bright planets, atmospheric effects, aircraft, balloons, and errors in judging distance and size have all been suggested as possible contributors. The difficulty is that a historical sighting cannot easily be reconstructed: the original witnesses are no longer available for detailed modern interviews, and there is no physical evidence confirming what was seen.

The lasting importance of Boianai is therefore cultural as much as evidential. It became one of the classic UFO stories of the twentieth century because it combined a remote location, multiple witnesses, a respected missionary observer, and imagery that fitted the popular “flying saucer” era.

## The Lake Murray Monster: a dinosaur in the swamps?

Another famous PNG mystery concerns reports from Lake Murray, the country’s largest lake. In 1999, newspaper reports described villagers and later other witnesses claiming to have seen a large unknown animal in the lake area. Descriptions included a long neck, long tail, large body, and a resemblance to a prehistoric reptile. The creature became known among cryptozoology enthusiasts as the “Lake Murray Monster”.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.genesispark.com/exhibits/evidence/cryptozoological/the-therapod-enclosure/the-lake-murray-monster-of-papua-new-guinea/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: genesispark.com">[genesispark.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">genesispark.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Lake Murray Monster of PNG &#124; Genesis Park</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Lake Murray Monster of PNG &#124; Genesis Park</span></span></span>

The attraction of the story is obvious: a huge, unknown animal in a remote wetland seems to fit the idea of a surviving prehistoric creature. However, there is no confirmed specimen, photograph, or scientific evidence proving the existence of such an animal.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.genesispark.com/exhibits/evidence/cryptozoological/the-therapod-enclosure/the-lake-murray-monster-of-papua-new-guinea/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: genesispark.com">[genesispark.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">genesispark.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Lake Murray Monster of PNG &#124; Genesis Park</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Lake Murray Monster of PNG &#124; Genesis Park</span></span></span>

More conventional possibilities include misidentified crocodiles, large monitor lizards, unusual views of known animals, or stories becoming enlarged through retelling. Lake environments can be especially deceptive because distance, water movement, vegetation, and poor visibility make size estimates difficult.

Even without proof, the Lake Murray Monster remains part of PNG’s strange-history tradition because it connects modern wildlife mystery with a very real feature of the country: enormous areas of difficult-to-explore habitat where biodiversity discoveries still occur.

## Strange lights, meteors, and objects in the sky

PNG has also produced a wider tradition of mysterious lights in the sky. Some reports have been connected to UFO investigations, while others may involve ordinary astronomical or atmospheric events. ## Spirits, forests, and the folklore of the unknown

Not all PNG mysteries involve objects in the sky or unknown animals. Many are rooted in traditional beliefs about unseen forces. Anthropological research has recorded beliefs about forest beings, dangerous spirits, and supernatural encounters among different PNG communities. These traditions often reflect practical concerns about entering unfamiliar environments and maintaining relationships between humans, ancestors, and the landscape.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1835-9310.1993.tb00177.x" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: onlinelibrary.wiley.com">[Wiley Online Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">onlinelibrary.wiley.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Online Library Forest and Demons in the Papua New Guinea Highlands</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wiley Online LibraryForest and Demons in the Papua New Guinea Highlands - Sillitoe - 1993 - The Australian Journal of Anthropology - Wile...</span></span></span>

## Why Papua New Guinea’s Fortean history still matters

Papua New Guinea’s strange reports endure because they sit at the meeting point of evidence and imagination. The Boianai sighting raises questions about witness reliability and unexplained aerial observations. The Lake Murray Monster reflects the fascination with unknown life in remote ecosystems. Spirit traditions show how landscapes can become filled with stories that preserve memory and meaning.

The country’s Fortean appeal does not depend on proving every mystery true or false. Its value lies in showing how people respond when they encounter something unusual: they investigate, record, interpret, debate, and sometimes transform uncertainty into legend. In Papua New Guinea, the unknown is not only found in the unexplored forest or the night sky; it is also found in the stories people create around the places they call home.

      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
