## Introduction

Bahla’s reputation as Oman’s “city of mystery” comes from generations of stories about jinn, magic, and strange encounters rather than from any verified supernatural event. The oasis town in Al Dakhiliyah governorate became famous in popular memory as a place where unseen beings were believed to inhabit old walls, abandoned houses, and the surrounding desert landscape. Reports and retellings describe tales of sorcery, unusual creatures, transformations, and encounters with jinn, creating an atmosphere that has made Bahla one of Oman’s best-known folklore locations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://qantara.de/en/article/bahla-city-djinns-magical-myths-haunt-ancient-omani-oasis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qantara.de">[Qantara]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qantara.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bahla: &#x27;City of djinns&#x27;: Oman oasis Bahla haunted by djinns &#124; Qantara.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bahla: &#x27;City of djinns&#x27;: Oman oasis Bahla haunted by djinns &#124; Qantara.deNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

The strange reputation of Bahla sits alongside a very real historical setting. Its vast mud-brick fort, oasis settlement, and defensive walls reflect centuries of human engineering and political power, especially during the period when the Banu Nebhan made the town an important centre between the 12th and 15th centuries. The contrast between a carefully built historic landscape and stories of invisible forces helping to shape it is what gives Bahla its lasting appeal as a piece of Omani folklore.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/433" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Bahla Fort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreBahla Fort - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

## Stories of jinn, magic, and unusual encounters

### Why Bahla became linked with the unseen world

In Omani and wider Arab traditions, jinn are understood as supernatural beings distinct from humans and angels. Stories about them often connect them with isolated places: deserts, ruins, caves, abandoned buildings, and locations where humans feel separated from ordinary life. In Bahla, these ideas became attached to the town’s old architecture and remote oasis setting, helping create a reputation that continues into the present.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://qantara.de/en/article/bahla-city-djinns-magical-myths-haunt-ancient-omani-oasis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qantara.de">[Qantara]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qantara.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bahla: &#x27;City of djinns&#x27;: Oman oasis Bahla haunted by djinns &#124; Qantara.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bahla: &#x27;City of djinns&#x27;: Oman oasis Bahla haunted by djinns &#124; Qantara.deNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

Many Bahla legends follow familiar folklore patterns found across the region. They involve mysterious forces appearing at night, people encountering beings they cannot explain, or individuals possessing unusual knowledge or abilities. Modern accounts of Bahla’s legends mention stories of animals behaving strangely, people being transformed, and magical practices associated with the town. These accounts are part of oral tradition and cultural storytelling rather than documented evidence of supernatural activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2023/11/city-jinn-magical-myths-haunt-ancient-omani-oasis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: al-monitor.com">[Al-Monitor]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">al-monitor.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">City of jinn&#x27;: magical myths haunt ancient Omani oasis</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Al-Monitor&#x27;City of jinn&#x27;: magical myths haunt ancient Omani oasis - AL-MONITOR: The Middle Eastʼs leading independent news source since 2012...</span></span></span>

The most famous element is not a single recorded “case” but a collection of stories that reinforce the same idea: Bahla is a place where the boundary between the visible and invisible worlds feels unusually close. For believers, such stories preserve memories of real encounters and old knowledge. For sceptics, they show how unfamiliar landscapes and inherited beliefs can shape the way people interpret unusual experiences.

### The wall built by jinn: a legend around a real monument

One of Bahla’s best-known legends concerns the town’s enormous defensive wall. Folklore tells of two sisters, Maitha and Gaitha, who were said to have knowledge of magic and who called upon jinn to construct the wall in a single night as protection for the settlement. The story transforms a remarkable human achievement into something almost impossible, giving the fortifications a supernatural explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/oman/omans-bahla-fort---where-civilisations-vie-with-legends-1.85764430" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gulfnews.com">[Gulf News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gulfnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Gulf News Oman’s Bahla fort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Gulf NewsOman’s Bahla fort - where civilisations vie with legendsFebruary 16, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 16, 2022</span></span></span>

The historical evidence points in another direction. UNESCO describes Bahla Fort and its surrounding oasis as an outstanding example of a medieval Islamic fortified settlement, with its walls, towers, mud-brick buildings, and water-management systems showing the skills of the people who built and maintained it. The legend does not explain how the wall was actually made, but it reveals how communities often attach extraordinary stories to impressive monuments whose scale seems beyond ordinary effort.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/433" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Bahla Fort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreBahla Fort - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

This pattern is common in folklore worldwide: ancient bridges, castles, roads, and temples are frequently attributed to giants, spirits, gods, or magical beings. In Bahla’s case, the jinn story adds a layer of wonder to a structure that is already remarkable through human history alone.

## How Bahla’s reputation developed over time

### From historic oasis to “city of magic”

Bahla was not originally famous only for supernatural stories. It was a prosperous oasis settlement surrounded by agricultural land, protected by extensive walls, and connected with important political and religious developments in medieval Oman. Its fort and settlement were recognised internationally when Bahla Fort became a UNESCO World Heritage Site, highlighting the town’s architectural and historical importance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/433" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Bahla Fort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreBahla Fort - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

Over time, however, the town accumulated another identity. Popular descriptions began presenting Bahla as a place associated with magic, sorcery, and jinn. Some visitors and writers have repeated stories that certain people once avoided the area because of its supernatural reputation. The persistence of these accounts shows how folklore can become attached to a location and influence how outsiders imagine it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://qantara.de/en/article/bahla-city-djinns-magical-myths-haunt-ancient-omani-oasis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qantara.de">[Qantara]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qantara.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bahla: &#x27;City of djinns&#x27;: Oman oasis Bahla haunted by djinns &#124; Qantara.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bahla: &#x27;City of djinns&#x27;: Oman oasis Bahla haunted by djinns &#124; Qantara.deNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

The growth of Bahla’s mysterious image was also helped by the visual character of the town itself. Abandoned mud-brick structures, narrow lanes, old walls, and desert surroundings create an environment that naturally invites storytelling. A place does not need confirmed paranormal events to become mysterious; atmosphere, memory, and repeated narratives can be enough.

### Legends as social memory

Bahla’s jinn traditions are valuable because they preserve how earlier generations explained uncertainty. Before modern science and technology transformed everyday life, many communities used stories of unseen beings to interpret strange sounds, unexplained events, illness, danger, or unusual behaviour.

These traditions also carried social messages. Stories about jinn often encouraged respect for certain places, warned people against arrogance, and reinforced ideas about the limits of human understanding. A tale about entering a forbidden area at night or disturbing hidden forces could function as both entertainment and a cultural warning.

Seen in this way, Bahla’s legends are not simply “ghost stories”. They are part of a broader system of beliefs about landscape, morality, memory, and the relationship between humans and the unknown.

## Folklore, belief, and modern explanations

### What believers and sceptics see differently

For those who accept jinn traditions as part of their worldview, Bahla’s stories may represent a genuine connection between people and an unseen realm. The continued telling of these stories reflects the importance of spiritual ideas in local culture and the way religious and folk traditions can overlap.

A sceptical interpretation focuses on how legends form and survive. Old settlements contain many features that can inspire mystery: strange echoes, shadows, unfamiliar animal noises, and buildings altered by time. Human perception is also strongly influenced by expectation. A person who has heard that a location is associated with spirits may interpret an ordinary event differently from someone without that background.

Neither approach removes the cultural importance of the stories. Whether understood as belief, metaphor, or folklore, Bahla’s legends show how communities create meaning around places that feel unusual.

### Why the mystery remains part of Bahla’s identity

Bahla’s supernatural reputation has become inseparable from its wider historical character. The town is not remembered only because of legends, and it is not remembered only because of its fort. Its fascination comes from the combination of both: a genuine historic landscape surrounded by stories of invisible powers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/433" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Bahla Fort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreBahla Fort - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

The enduring appeal of Bahla lies in that tension. The walls, towers, and oasis can be studied through archaeology and history, while the jinn stories belong to the realm of folklore and cultural memory. Together they create one of Oman’s most distinctive mystery traditions: a place where human craftsmanship and supernatural imagination have become woven into the same landscape.
