## Introduction

Selma, the legendary lake monster of Seljordsvatnet in Telemark, is [Norway]({{ 'norways-strangest-mysteries-between/' | relative_url }})’s best-known lake creature story. For more than 250 years, people have reported seeing a large, snake-like animal, unusual movements on the water, and unexplained disturbances in the lake. The evidence, however, remains a mixture of eyewitness accounts, folklore, ambiguous photographs, instrument readings and local tradition rather than proof of an unknown animal.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seljord.kommune.no/tenester/velkomen-til-seljord/sjoormen-og-sjoormtarnet" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seljord.kommune.no">[seljord.kommune.no]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seljord.kommune.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sjøormen og Sjøormtårnet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">January 29, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 29, 2025</span></span></span>

The mystery endures because Selma sits in an unusual middle ground. The reports are too numerous and culturally important to dismiss as a single invented tale, yet the investigations have never produced a confirmed creature, body, clear image or biological trace. The Seljordsvatnet case is therefore less a story of a monster being found than a long-running example of how landscape, observation, science and folklore can combine to create one of Norway’s most famous unexplained traditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seljord.kommune.no/tenester/velkomen-til-seljord/sjoormen-og-sjoormtarnet" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seljord.kommune.no">[seljord.kommune.no]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seljord.kommune.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sjøormen og Sjøormtårnet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">January 29, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 29, 2025</span></span></span>

## Sightings from Seljordsvatnet

Selma is the modern nickname for the Seljordsormen, the “sea serpent” said to inhabit Seljordsvatnet, a lake in Seljord, Telemark. Although often compared with Scotland’s Loch Ness monster, the tradition is older than modern lake-monster tourism. Written accounts go back to the eighteenth century, with the first commonly cited report dating from 1750. According to the story, Gunleik Andersson Verpe from Bø encountered a sea serpent while rowing across the lake, and the creature overturned one of his boats.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seljord.kommune.no/tenester/velkomen-til-seljord/sjoormen-og-sjoormtarnet" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seljord.kommune.no">[seljord.kommune.no]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seljord.kommune.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sjøormen og Sjøormtårnet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">January 29, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 29, 2025</span></span></span>

Later descriptions vary considerably. Some witnesses have described a long, dark form moving beneath the surface; others have reported humps, ripples, a head-like shape or a creature creating a wake without an obvious boat or animal being visible. This variation is important: the reports create a consistent theme of something unusual in the water, but they do not produce a single stable description of what that thing might be.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://telemarkshistorier.no/kultur-og-tradisjon/sjoormen-i-seljordsvatnet/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: telemarkshistorier.no">[Telemarkshistorier]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">telemarkshistorier.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sjøormen i Seljordsvatnet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sjøormen i Seljordsvatnet - TelemarkshistorierSeptember 9, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2025</span></span></span>

Modern sightings have often followed a familiar pattern seen in lake-monster reports elsewhere: calm weather, still water and a sudden disturbance that appears too large or unusual for the observer to identify immediately. Local accounts and tourism material frequently describe the best chances of seeing Selma as periods when the lake surface is flat and reflections or movements are easier to notice.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://seljordcamping.no/sjoormen.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seljordcamping.no">[seljordcamping.no]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seljordcamping.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sjøormen i Seljordsvatnet – Seljord Camping</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sjøormen i Seljordsvatnet – Seljord Camping</span></span></span>

One of the most discussed modern incidents came during the 1998 international search for Selma. The expedition used equipment including sonar, underwater cameras and remote-operated vehicles. During the search, investigators reported unusual sonar contacts and surface disturbances, but these did not lead to a confirmed identification of a large unknown animal.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.xprojectmagazine.com/archives/cryptozoology/gustsearch2.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: xprojectmagazine.com">[xprojectmagazine.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">xprojectmagazine.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">X-Project: GUST98 Expedition Scrapbook</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">August 29, 1998...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 29, 1998</span></span></span>

A claimed photograph opportunity also emerged during the 1998 expedition, when expedition leader Jan-Ove Sundberg reported photographing unusual waves and ripples near the shore. The event attracted media attention, but the images did not establish the existence of a lake monster.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dagbladet.no/nyheter/kan-ha-bilde-av-sjoormen/65472702" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dagbladet.no">[Dagbladet]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dagbladet.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Kan ha bilde av sjøormen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Kan ha bilde av sjøormenAugust 9, 1998...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 9, 1998</span></span></span>

## Evidence, misidentifications, and debate

The strongest evidence for Selma is not a physical discovery but the continuity of testimony. Generations of residents and visitors have claimed to observe something unusual in Seljordsvatnet, and the story has survived through newspapers, local histories and repeated investigations. For believers, the sheer persistence of reports suggests that there may be a real phenomenon behind the legend.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seljord.kommune.no/tenester/velkomen-til-seljord/sjoormen-og-sjoormtarnet" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seljord.kommune.no">[seljord.kommune.no]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seljord.kommune.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sjøormen og Sjøormtårnet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">January 29, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 29, 2025</span></span></span>

However, eyewitness accounts alone cannot determine what caused the sightings. A moving shape on a large lake can be produced by many ordinary causes, including waves, floating debris, birds, fish activity, logs, boat wakes or animals seen from unusual angles. The problem is especially difficult with lake monsters because the most dramatic features — a long body, humps or a strange wake — are often observed briefly and at a distance.

The physical evidence has also remained inconclusive.

**Sonar searches:** The 1998 expedition generated excitement after reports of large sonar echoes. Such signals can show that something reflected sound waves, but sonar alone cannot identify an animal. Objects such as groups of fish, underwater structures or other unknown sources can create confusing readings. The expedition’s own reported findings did not become accepted evidence of Selma’s existence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dagbladet.no/nyheter/er-seljord-ormen-funnet/65472440" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dagbladet.no">[Dagbladet]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dagbladet.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Er Seljord-ormen funnet?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Er Seljord-ormen funnet?...</span></span></span>

**Photographs and video:** Several images and recordings have been promoted over the years, but none has provided a clear, independently verified view of an unknown creature. This is a recurring issue in cryptozoology: unusual images can be interesting without being scientifically decisive.

**Physical remains:** A 1983 discovery of a 3.65-metre snake-like carcass near Seljordsvatnet was sometimes discussed as a possible monster clue. However, it did not establish the existence of a giant unknown lake animal, and remains found in such cases are often open to alternative explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://telemarkshistorier.no/kultur-og-tradisjon/sjoormen-i-seljordsvatnet/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: telemarkshistorier.no">[Telemarkshistorier]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">telemarkshistorier.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sjøormen i Seljordsvatnet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sjøormen i Seljordsvatnet - TelemarkshistorierSeptember 9, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2025</span></span></span>

### Why a hidden giant animal remains disputed

The lake itself is another important part of the debate. Seljordsvatnet is a substantial Norwegian lake, but sceptics argue that a breeding population of very large unknown animals would face serious ecological challenges, including finding enough food and remaining undetected for centuries. Local discussions of the legend have highlighted these questions while acknowledging that unusual observations continue to be reported.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.visittelemark.info/seljord/ting-a-gjera/kunst-og-kultur/sjoormen" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visittelemark.info">[Visit Telemark]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visittelemark.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visit Telemark Sjøormen i Seljordsvatnet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit TelemarkSjøormen i Seljordsvatnet - Seljord...</span></span></span>

Supporters of the mystery argue that unexplained sightings deserve investigation and that not every report has an obvious explanation. Sceptics counter that the lack of a specimen, clear photographs, DNA evidence or repeatable scientific observations means the case remains a folklore and perception mystery rather than a zoological discovery.

The most balanced view is that Selma represents a genuine tradition of reported experiences, but the evidence currently supports the existence of a powerful legend more strongly than the existence of an unknown animal.

## The monster as local culture

Whatever the truth behind the sightings, Selma has become a central part of Seljord’s identity. The creature appears in local symbolism and has been incorporated into tourism, including the municipality’s coat of arms, which features a sea serpent design adopted in 1989.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lokalhistoriewiki.no/index.php?title=Seljordsormen" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lokalhistoriewiki.no">[Lokalhistoriewiki]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lokalhistoriewiki.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Seljordsormen – lokalhistoriewiki.no</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Seljordsormen – lokalhistoriewiki.no...</span></span></span>

This cultural role helps explain why Selma remains significant even without a definitive discovery. The lake monster connects modern Norway with older traditions of mysterious creatures in waterways, where unfamiliar sights and dangerous environments were often explained through stories. In this sense, Selma is not only a claimed animal but also a piece of living folklore.

The case also shows how mysteries evolve. Early reports described a threatening water creature; later generations searched with cameras, sonar and scientific methods; today the legend survives as a mixture of tourism, local pride, eyewitness mystery and debate over how humans interpret uncertain events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seljord.kommune.no/tenester/velkomen-til-seljord/sjoormen-og-sjoormtarnet" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seljord.kommune.no">[seljord.kommune.no]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seljord.kommune.no</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sjøormen og Sjøormtårnet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">January 29, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 29, 2025</span></span></span>

Selma has never been found in the sense of being captured, identified or scientifically confirmed. Yet the absence of a final answer is part of what has kept the Seljordsvatnet monster alive: it remains a Norwegian mystery where evidence, imagination and uncertainty continue to share the same shoreline.
