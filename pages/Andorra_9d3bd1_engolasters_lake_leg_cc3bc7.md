## Introduction

Engolasters Lake is one of [Andorra]({{ 'why-does-andorra-feel-so-uncanny/' | relative_url }})'s best-known places of legend because it combines a real mountain landscape with a surprisingly rich body of folklore. The best-known tale claims that a village once stood where the lake now lies, only to be swallowed by a catastrophic flood after its people rejected a desperate beggar. Alongside that moral story sits a darker tradition: witches were said to gather at the lake after nightfall, bathing naked and punishing anyone who dared to spy on them by transforming them into black cats. None of these stories is supported by historical evidence, but together they have become one of the country's most enduring examples of how folklore can attach itself to a striking natural landmark. The legends remain part of local tourism, oral tradition and Andorra's wider reputation as a landscape shaped as much by stories as by glaciers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[visitandorra.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;EngolastersTo punish them, the witches turned all “voyeurs” into black cats. ## The beggar, the merciful girl and the flood

The drowned village legend belongs to a widespread family of European moral tales in which generosity is rewarded while selfishness brings disaster. In the Engolasters version, a poor traveller or beggar arrives at a prosperous settlement near the lake's present location and asks for food. House after house refuses him. Some versions even describe the villagers mocking or driving him away.

Only a young woman takes pity on the stranger and shares her bread. Before leaving, the beggar quietly warns her to flee with her family without looking back. During the night an overwhelming flood engulfs the settlement, leaving a mountain lake where the village once stood. The compassionate girl survives because she obeys the warning, while the rest of the community perishes beneath the water.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://multicoloreddiary.blogspot.com/2018/12/land-of-witches-following-folktales.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: multicoloreddiary.blogspot.com">[multicoloreddiary.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">multicoloreddiary.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The lake becomes a favorite bathing place of Andorran witches.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Land of witches (Following folktales around the world 95.10 Dec 2018 — That night a flood drowns the entire town, and Lake Engolasters is...</span></span></span>

Different retellings identify the mysterious traveller in different ways. Some simply call him a beggar or pilgrim, while others reinterpret him as Christ travelling in disguise, turning the story into a Christian lesson about charity and hospitality. The variations reflect the way oral traditions evolve over generations rather than preserving a single fixed text.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Engolasters" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lake Engolasters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lake Engolasters</span></span></span>

Like many drowned-city legends across Europe, the point is less to explain geology than to explain morality. The lake becomes a permanent reminder that wealth without compassion ultimately destroys itself.

## Witches, black cats and night-time boundaries

The second major Engolasters tradition shifts from divine punishment to supernatural danger. According to local folklore, the lake became a gathering place for witches after dark. They were said to meet beside the water, dance and bathe naked beneath the night sky, hidden from ordinary people.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[Visit Andorra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;EngolastersTo punish them, the witches turned all “voyeurs” into black cats. Curiosity, however, carried a price. Young men supposedly climbed to the lake hoping to watch the secret gatherings. If a witch caught a voyeur, she would transform him into a black cat. ### Why black cats?

The black-cat motif is particularly memorable because it reverses a familiar European belief. Elsewhere, witches often keep black cats as magical companions or familiars. At Engolasters, the punishment is more personal: the intruder becomes the cat himself. That twist turns simple curiosity into a permanent loss of human identity, making the warning far more dramatic.

## How mountain lakes become moral landscapes

Nothing in the geology of Engolasters Lake suggests that it conceals the remains of a real medieval settlement. The lake occupies a glacial basin later modified by twentieth-century engineering, including the construction of a dam for hydroelectric generation. Archaeological evidence for a drowned village has never emerged.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/content/dam/visitandorra/nl/assets/documents/cfs/itinerary/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/13-engolasters-lake-eng.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[visitandorra.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">, witches met secretly here to bat- he naked in the...Read more...</span></span></span>

Instead, folklorists recognise the story as part of a broader narrative pattern found throughout Europe. Lakes and marshes frequently become the imagined graves of sinful towns, greedy communities or individuals who fail a test of generosity. Such stories explain striking landscapes through moral cause and effect rather than natural processes.

Engolasters is especially effective because the real setting already feels unusual. The quiet mountain water, surrounding forest and changing weather encourage the imagination, while the isolated location naturally lends itself to tales of nocturnal gatherings and hidden dangers. The legends transform an attractive walking destination into a place where every feature seems to carry symbolic meaning.

## Why the legend still matters

Today, most visitors encounter Engolasters as an accessible hiking destination rather than a place of fear. Official tourism material openly embraces the folklore, presenting the drowned village and witch stories as part of the area's cultural heritage alongside its natural scenery and hydroelectric history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[Visit Andorra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;EngolastersTo punish them, the witches turned all “voyeurs” into black cats. Luckily, the witches aband...</span></span></span>

From a Fortean perspective, the value of Engolasters lies not in proving that witches danced by the lake or that a village truly lies beneath its waters. Its importance comes from showing how folklore, religion and landscape reinforce one another. A mountain lake becomes a lesson about charity, a warning against forbidden curiosity and a stage for supernatural encounters, all while remaining a perfectly real place that thousands of walkers visit each year.

That combination of physical reality and enduring oral tradition explains why Engolasters remains one of Andorra's defining strange places. The lake invites visitors to enjoy its scenery while also imagining the invisible stories said to linger beneath its calm surface.
