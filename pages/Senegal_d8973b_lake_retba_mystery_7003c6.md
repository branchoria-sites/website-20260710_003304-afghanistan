## Introduction

Lake Retba, known locally and internationally as the Pink Lake or Lac Rose, is one of [Senegal]({{ 'senegals-strange-stories-beyond-the/' | relative_url }})’s most visually unusual natural landmarks. Its strange appearance has often made it feel like a place from folklore: a lake that seems to have been coloured deliberately rather than formed by ordinary geology and biology. The explanation, however, is a real natural process. The pink and reddish tones come mainly from salt-loving microorganisms, especially the microscopic algae-like organism *Dunaliella salina*, which produces protective pigments when living in the lake’s extreme salty conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/pink-lake-loses-its-rosy-hue-150419/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Pink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA SciencePink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue - NASA ScienceOctober 6, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2022</span></span></span>

The mystery of Lake Retba is therefore not that the lake is supernatural, but that an everyday combination of salt, sunlight, evaporation and microscopic life creates a landscape that looks almost impossible. Its changing colours, unusual chemistry and role in Senegalese tourism have helped make it part of the country’s wider record of strange and memorable places.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lakeretba.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lakeretba.com">[Lake Retba]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lakeretba.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lake RetbaLake Retba (Lac Rose) - The Pink Lake of Senegal...</span></span></span>

## The unusual appearance of Lake Retba

### Why does the water look pink?

Lake Retba lies near Dakar on Senegal’s Cap-Vert Peninsula, separated from the Atlantic Ocean by a narrow belt of dunes. Its high salt concentration creates an extreme environment where relatively few organisms can survive. Under these conditions, *Dunaliella salina* thrives because it is specially adapted to salty water.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://encyclopaediaafricana.com/lake-retba/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: encyclopaediaafricana.com">[Encyclopaedia Africana]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">encyclopaediaafricana.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Encyclopaedia Africana LAKE RETBA</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Encyclopaedia AfricanaLAKE RETBA - SENEGAL - Encyclopaedia Africana...</span></span></span>

The colour comes from pigments produced by these microorganisms. Although *Dunaliella salina* is a type of green microalgae, it can accumulate carotenoid pigments, including beta-carotene, when exposed to intense sunlight and high salt stress. These orange-red pigments help protect the cells from harsh conditions and also alter the colour seen across the lake surface.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/pink-lake-loses-its-rosy-hue-150419/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Pink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA SciencePink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue - NASA ScienceOctober 6, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2022</span></span></span>

This creates one of nature’s most striking visual tricks: the same conditions that make the lake difficult for most life also encourage the tiny organisms that give it its famous appearance. The pink colour is strongest when evaporation increases salinity and sunlight is intense, particularly during drier periods. During wetter months, when rainfall dilutes the lake, the colour can become weaker or change noticeably.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lakeretba.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lakeretba.com">[Lake Retba]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lakeretba.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lake RetbaLake Retba (Lac Rose) - The Pink Lake of Senegal...</span></span></span>

### A lake that can change its own identity

Visitors sometimes expect Lake Retba to look permanently bright pink, but the reality is more complicated. The lake’s appearance depends on several environmental factors, including water levels, salt concentration, sunlight and microbial conditions. This means photographs taken at different times can show anything from vivid pink shades to more muted colours.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/pink-lake-loses-its-rosy-hue-150419/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Pink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA SciencePink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue - NASA ScienceOctober 6, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2022</span></span></span>

This variability has added to the lake’s sense of mystery. A traveller who arrives after heavy rains may wonder whether the famous “Pink Lake” has disappeared, while another visitor during a dry, sunny period may see the dramatic colour that made the site famous. In 2022, NASA’s Earth Observatory highlighted how unusually wet conditions turned Lake Retba’s waters dark blue-green rather than pink, showing that the phenomenon is dynamic rather than fixed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/pink-lake-loses-its-rosy-hue-150419/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Pink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA SciencePink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue - NASA ScienceOctober 6, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2022</span></span></span>

## Science behind the pink colour

### The role of salt and extreme conditions

Lake Retba’s colour cannot be separated from its chemistry. The lake is hypersaline, meaning it contains far more dissolved salt than ordinary lakes. In some areas its salt concentration has been reported as comparable to or exceeding that of the Dead Sea. The intense evaporation caused by Senegal’s climate concentrates minerals and creates the conditions that allow salt-loving organisms to dominate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lakeretba.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lakeretba.com">[Lake Retba]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lakeretba.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lake RetbaLake Retba (Lac Rose) - The Pink Lake of Senegal...</span></span></span>

The relationship between salt and colour is the key mechanism:

* High salinity limits many competing organisms.
* Salt-tolerant microorganisms such as *Dunaliella salina* survive where others cannot.
* Strong sunlight encourages pigment production.
* The accumulated pigments give the water its pink, red or orange appearance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/pink-lake-loses-its-rosy-hue-150419/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Pink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA SciencePink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue - NASA ScienceOctober 6, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2022</span></span></span>

The lake also demonstrates that “strange-looking” does not necessarily mean unexplained. Many of Earth’s most unusual landscapes are produced by ordinary physical and biological processes operating under unusual conditions.

### More than one microscopic world

Although *Dunaliella salina* is the main organism associated with Lake Retba’s pink colour, scientific studies have also found other specialised microorganisms living in the lake’s harsh environment. Research has identified salt-tolerant bacteria from Lake Retba sediments, including the species *Desulfohalobium retbaense*, showing that the lake supports a specialised microbial ecosystem rather than being a lifeless pool of salt water.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.microbiologyresearch.org/content/journal/ijsem/10.1099/00207713" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: microbiologyresearch.org">[microbiologyresearch.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">microbiologyresearch.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Microbiology SocietyJanuary 1, 1991...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 1, 1991</span></span></span>

This matters because the lake’s unusual appearance is not caused by a single “colouring agent” but by a whole environmental system. The pink water is the visible surface expression of a much larger interaction between microorganisms, minerals, climate and geography.

## Mystery, tourism, and changing perceptions

Lake Retba became famous because it occupies an unusual space between science and wonder. To visitors, a naturally pink lake appears almost like a fantasy landscape. To researchers, it is an example of how microorganisms can transform an environment on a large visible scale. Both perspectives come from the same physical reality: a rare ecological balance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/pink-lake-loses-its-rosy-hue-150419/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Pink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA SciencePink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue - NASA ScienceOctober 6, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2022</span></span></span>

The lake has also become an important part of local economic life. Alongside tourism, salt harvesting has long been a major activity around Lake Retba. Workers collect salt from the water, which accumulates because of the lake’s extreme salinity. This human connection adds another layer to the lake’s strange appeal: it is not simply a scenic curiosity but a working landscape shaped by both nature and communities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/pink-lake-loses-its-rosy-hue-150419/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Pink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA SciencePink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue - NASA ScienceOctober 6, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2022</span></span></span>

### When the “mystery” changes

Lake Retba shows how natural wonders can become misunderstood when photographs capture only one moment. Images of intensely pink water can create the impression of a permanent anomaly, while the real story is more subtle. The lake changes because its colour depends on environmental balance. Rainfall, flooding and changes in water conditions can temporarily reduce the effect.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/pink-lake-loses-its-rosy-hue-150419/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Pink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA SciencePink Lake Loses its Rosy Hue - NASA ScienceOctober 6, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2022</span></span></span>

For Senegal’s strange-history landscape, Lake Retba is an important example of a different kind of mystery. It is not a case of a hidden creature, unexplained force or supernatural event.
