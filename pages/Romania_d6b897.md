## Introduction

The important question is not whether every unusual Romanian story is literally true. Many cannot be verified, and some have ordinary explanations. The lasting fascination comes from the way these accounts reveal how people interpret uncertainty: a strange light becomes a sign, a forest becomes a place of mystery, and an old folk belief adapts to the age of cameras, television, and the internet.

## Why Romania became a centre of strange stories

Romanias Fortean reputation comes from the meeting point of several traditions. The country has a long rural folklore heritage, a landscape of mountains and forests that naturally encourages mystery, and a modern history in which unusual events were often recorded through newspapers, amateur investigators, or local storytelling rather than formal scientific study. Romanian folklore contains many supernatural figures and beliefs, including the strigoi, a revenant or restless dead figure whose stories have been studied as part of traditional culture rather than treated simply as vampire tales.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cambridgescholars.com/product/978" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cambridgescholars.com">[Cambridge Scholars Publishing]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cambridgescholars.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cambridge Scholars PublishingFolkloric Aspects of the Romanian Imaginary and Myth - Cambridge Scholars Publishing...</span></span></span>

This background matters because modern paranormal stories in Romania often borrow older patterns. A mysterious presence in the woods, unexplained illness after a strange encounter, or fear of the returning dead are not isolated ideas imported from modern horror fiction. ## The Hoia-Baciu forest and the famous UFO photograph

The best-known Romanian mystery is the Hoia-Baciu forest, located near Cluj-Napoca in Transylvania. The forest became internationally famous after an incident on 18 August 1968, when military technician Emil Barnea photographed what he described as a disc-shaped object above a clearing. The images became among Romanias most discussed UFO cases and remain a central part of the countrys modern Fortean identity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.inverted.world/news/story/tale-hoia-baciu-forest-romania" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: inverted.world">[Inverted World]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">inverted.world</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Disc Over the Crooked Wood: How a Romanian Officer Risked His Career to Print Four Photos the State Couldn&#x27;t Explain &#124; Inverted World...</span></span></span>

The appeal of the case comes from the combination of ordinary circumstances and extraordinary claims. Barnea was not presenting a fantasy story created for entertainment; he was a named witness who produced photographs. Supporters of the case have argued that the images show an unexplained object, while sceptics have suggested possibilities such as a thrown object, a model, or photographic effects. The photographs have never provided enough evidence to settle the argument conclusively.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.inverted.world/news/story/tale-hoia-baciu-forest-romania" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: inverted.world">[Inverted World]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">inverted.world</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Disc Over the Crooked Wood: How a Romanian Officer Risked His Career to Print Four Photos the State Couldn&#x27;t Explain &#124; Inverted World...</span></span></span>

The forest itself adds to the legend. Visitors have reported strange feelings, unusual lights, equipment problems, and a sense that the location is somehow different from normal woodland. These claims have helped create its reputation as one of the worlds most famous haunted forests, although there is no accepted scientific evidence that the area contains paranormal effects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/travel/2025/oct/30/worlds-most-haunted-forest-transylvania-romania" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tour guide Marius Lazin, who has led night walks there for over a decade, recounts chilling tales of disappearances, including a five-yea...</span></span></span>

## Strange skies: UFO reports and natural wonders

Romania has also produced a long history of unusual sky reports. UFO investigators in the country have collected accounts ranging from strange lights to unidentified objects, especially during the twentieth century when aviation, satellites, and military activity created new opportunities for unusual observations. Romanian UFO groups have documented many such reports, although a recorded sighting is not the same thing as evidence of extraterrestrial activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dni.org.ro/istoria-aparitiilor-ozn-in-romania/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dni.org.ro">[DNI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dni.org.ro</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">December 2, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 2, 2015</span></span></span>

Some famous cases have alternative explanations that fit ordinary phenomena. Bright lights in the sky can be caused by aircraft, planets, meteors, atmospheric effects, balloons, or human technology. This does not make every report worthless; rather, it shows why careful investigation matters. A witness may accurately describe what they saw while still misunderstanding what caused it.

Romania has also experienced genuinely dramatic astronomical events. On 7 January 2015, a very bright fireball crossed the Romanian sky. Researchers analysed recordings and concluded it was a rare superbolide, a powerful meteor event produced by a space rock entering Earths atmosphere. Unlike many UFO stories, this event became a scientific investigation because observations could be compared and measured.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.03968" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The January 7, 2015, superbolide over Romania and structural diversity of meter-sized asteroidsFebruary 13, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2015</span></span></span>

The contrast between the two types of stories is central to Forteana: some mysteries remain uncertain because evidence is limited, while others become understandable once better observations are available.

## Ghosts, revenants and Romanias living folklore

Long before modern UFOs, Romanian communities told stories about spirits, magical beings, and the return of the dead. The strigoi is among the most famous examples. Although often simplified internationally as a vampire, the traditional figure has a broader meaning connected with beliefs about restless spirits, death, illness, and social order. Folklore researchers have examined Romanian accounts of strigoi as part of a wider system of cultural beliefs rather than as simple horror stories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=173013" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ceeol.com">[CEEOL]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ceeol.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">article detail</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">article detail</span></span></span>

These legends often explain misfortune or unusual experiences through supernatural language.
