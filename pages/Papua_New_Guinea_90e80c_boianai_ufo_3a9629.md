## Introduction

The 1959 Boianai UFO mystery remains [Papua New Guinea]({{ 'papua-new-guineas-strangest-stories/' | relative_url }})’s most famous unidentified aerial report because it combines several features that make a case historically unusual: a respected missionary witness, numerous local observers, written records made close to the event, and a dramatic claim of apparent interaction with a strange object. At the same time, it has never produced physical evidence, photographs, or measurements that would confirm what the witnesses saw. The lasting debate is therefore not simply whether people reported something unusual, but what those reports can reliably tell us.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/cases/father-gill-papua-new-guinea-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[ufouap.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Father Gill Papua New Guinea sighting &#124; Global UFO Archive &#124; Global UFO Archive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 26, 1959...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 26, 1959</span></span></span>

## The 1959 mission station sightings

The Boianai sightings took place at the Anglican mission station of Boianai in the Territory of Papua and New Guinea, then administered by Australia. The central figure was Reverend William Booth Gill, an Anglican missionary who recorded a series of unusual aerial observations during 1959. His reports focused especially on events in late June, including sightings on 21, 26, and 27 June.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-au-a703-580-1-1-part2-a703-580-1-1-part-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufotransparency.com.</span></span></span>

Gill’s own notes are an important part of the case because they were written as observations rather than only as later memories. In his report, he described himself as the main recorder of the events and distinguished between his personal opinions and the observational material collected from witnesses. The surviving report shows that Gill initially approached the subject cautiously rather than immediately claiming an extraterrestrial explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-au-a703-580-1-1-part2-a703-580-1-1-part-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufotransparency.com.</span></span></span>

The most famous episode occurred on 26 June 1959. Gill and other witnesses reported seeing a large object above the mission area, described in later accounts as a disc-like craft. The striking element of the story was the claim that figures appeared on top of the object and that gestures between the witnesses and the figures seemed to be exchanged. According to the accounts, Gill waved towards the object and believed the figures responded.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/cases/father-gill-papua-new-guinea-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[ufouap.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Father Gill Papua New Guinea sighting &#124; Global UFO Archive &#124; Global UFO Archive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 26, 1959...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 26, 1959</span></span></span>

This detail transformed Boianai from an ordinary “unidentified light” report into what UFO researchers call a close encounter case. However, the interaction element depends entirely on witness interpretation: there is no recording, instrument data, or independent physical trace showing that the object or figures were actually present.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/cases/father-gill-papua-new-guinea-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[ufouap.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Father Gill Papua New Guinea sighting &#124; Global UFO Archive &#124; Global UFO Archive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 26, 1959...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 26, 1959</span></span></span>

## Witnesses, records, and reported objects

One reason Boianai remains debated is that it was not based only on one person’s testimony. Gill collected accounts from mission workers and local residents, with later retellings often emphasising the large number of witnesses who reportedly saw the object. The Australian authorities also received reports and investigated the matter, leaving a documentary trail that makes the case more substantial than many folklore-style UFO stories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-au-a703-580-1-1-part2-a703-580-1-1-part-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufotransparency.com.</span></span></span>

The strongest evidence for the case is therefore documentary rather than physical:

* **Contemporary written records:** Gill produced reports describing dates, conditions, and witness accounts close to the time of the events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-au-a703-580-1-1-part2-a703-580-1-1-part-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufotransparency.com.</span></span></span>
* **Multiple witnesses:** The case involved several observers rather than a single isolated claim.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/cases/father-gill-papua-new-guinea-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[ufouap.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Father Gill Papua New Guinea sighting &#124; Global UFO Archive &#124; Global UFO Archive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 26, 1959...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 26, 1959</span></span></span>
* **A credible central witness:** Supporters often note that Gill was a missionary with a reputation for seriousness and that he did not begin as a committed UFO believer.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59report.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[ufologie.patrickgross.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo - UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, detailed report...</span></span></span>
* **A lack of physical confirmation:** No material object, photograph, radar record, or scientific measurement has emerged to verify the reported craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufouap.net/en/cases/father-gill-papua-new-guinea-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufouap.net">[ufouap.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufouap.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Father Gill Papua New Guinea sighting &#124; Global UFO Archive &#124; Global UFO Archive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 26, 1959...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 26, 1959</span></span></span>

The description of the object itself is also part of the debate. Witnesses reported lights and structured shapes, but translating a night-time observation into reliable information about size, distance, speed, or construction is extremely difficult. A bright object seen against a dark sky can appear solid and close even when it is distant, and human observers often struggle to judge scale without reference points.

## Explanations from believers and sceptics

### Why some researchers consider Boianai significant

UFO researchers have long regarded Boianai as an important historical case because it combines multiple witnesses with a detailed written record. The argument is not simply that many people saw something, but that the accounts contain specific features: an apparently structured object, repeated sightings over several nights, and reported responses between observers and the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://spookyvalley.com/events/ufo-1959-boianai-father-gill/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spookyvalley.com">[Spooky Valley]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spookyvalley.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Spooky Valley Father Gill PNG Sighting &#124; Spooky Valley</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Spooky ValleyFather Gill PNG Sighting &#124; Spooky ValleyJune 26, 1959...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 26, 1959</span></span></span>

Some investigators have argued that ordinary explanations account well for parts of the case but struggle with the main close encounter report. In particular, later researchers associated with J. Allen Hynek’s UFO investigations argued that bright stars or planets could explain some of the lesser lights reported around Boianai, while the larger object described during the main event remained harder to explain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59letters.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[ufologie.patrickgross.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo - UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, the letters...</span></span></span>

For believers, the case’s appeal lies in its apparent human element. The story is not just of a mysterious light in the sky, but of people who believed they briefly encountered something unfamiliar. That narrative quality has helped Boianai survive as part of Papua New Guinea’s modern strange-history record.

### Why sceptics remain unconvinced

Sceptical explanations focus on the limits of witness testimony. The Australian Royal Australian Air Force investigation considered whether natural phenomena, including planets and atmospheric effects, could explain parts of the reports. The importance of Boianai lies less in proving or disproving extraterrestrial visitation and more in showing how unusual experiences become historical mysteries.
